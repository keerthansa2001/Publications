# Research Publications

Research work by **Keerthan Suresh** and collaborators in machine learning, generative AI, and reverse engineering.

## Papers

### 1. Log Anomaly Detection Using Machine Learning

**Published:** *International Journal of Engineering Applied Sciences and Technology (IJEAST)*, Vol. 7, Issue 2, pp. 356–363 (2022).

This work investigates machine-learning-based anomaly detection in system and network logs. It evaluates Random Forest, Decision Tree, and Multinomial Naive Bayes classifiers, with natural language preprocessing for unstructured log messages.

- [Read the published paper (IJEAST)](https://www.ijeast.com/papers/356-363,%20Tesma0702,IJEAST,%2017429.pdf)

### 2. A Survey of Using Generative Models to Perform Reverse Engineering

**Type:** Coauthored research survey; journal/conference publication status not specified.

A comparative survey of four generative-model families—GANs, VAEs, Transformers/LLMs, and Diffusion Models—across software, hardware, and protocol reverse engineering. The work discusses binary decompilation, circuit reconstruction, protocol inference, and challenges involving hallucinations, interpretability, adversarial risks, and intellectual property.

**Authors:** Keerthan Suresh, Vipin Thandavamurthy Madhiazhagan, Gogineni Pavan Sai Srinivas, and Jonnagurukula Harshavardhan Reddy.
